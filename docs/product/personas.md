# Fictional target-audience personas

Updated: 2026-10-09. These are the fictional personas the developer envisions as the app's target audience, not interview-derived profiles. Teddy is the **provisional primary** because the current vision centers a tech-savvy movie viewer; Jenna is the **provisional secondary**. The owner has not confirmed that ordering. Each persona has a short scenario underneath, using the lecture's personal / daily role / skills / relevance structure without inventing unrelated biography.

## Teddy

| Aspect | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Personal | Mid-teens high-school student who loves movies. |
| Daily role | Student and movie viewer; other daily details are not supplied. |
| Education / skills | Tech-savvy, conversational English learned largely through movies; finds films, subtitles and information online. |
| Relevance | Science-fiction and historical dialogue can exceed his English; he dislikes slow internet and wasted time. |

### Scenario — understanding a difficult scene

Teddy finds an English SRT for a science-fiction or historical film, but cannot follow the complex dialogue. He uploads it, chooses a language, watches progress and compares difficult translated cues with the English before downloading. If the job stalls or wording seems wrong, he needs a clear retry path and cue context. Whether this saves enough time or improves his understanding remains unverified.

## Jenna

| Aspect | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Personal | Early thirties, lives with her family; family movie night is weekly. |
| Daily role | Organizes the weekly family movie night; occupation is not relevant to this use. |
| Education / skills | Advanced English; can find a movie and English SRT and upload the file. Formal education is not relevant to this use. |
| Relevance | Her parents do not understand English. She needs translated subtitles good enough for them to follow the plot, without correcting errors in the web app herself. |

### Scenario — preparing family movie night

Jenna has a movie and English SRT before her family's weekly viewing. She uploads the file, translates it into the family's mother tongue and downloads the result so her parents can follow the plot. She will not correct cues in the web app. Awkward wording or text placed in the wrong time slot could still confuse her parents; whether the unedited output is adequate remains unverified by human viewing.
